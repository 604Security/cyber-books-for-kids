# 📚 Cyber Books for Kids

A curated list of books that teach children and teens about online safety, privacy, codes and ciphers, and cybersecurity, sorted by age.

Want to add a book? See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Picture Books (Ages 3–7)

| Title | Author | Topic |
|-------|--------|-------|
| *Chicken Clicking* | Jeanne Willis & Tony Ross | Stranger danger online, online shopping |
| *#Goldilocks: A Hashtag Cautionary Tale* | Jeanne Willis & Tony Ross | Oversharing on social media |
| *Troll Stinks!* | Jeanne Willis & Tony Ross | Cyberbullying |
| *The Internet Is Like a Puddle* | Shona Innes | Internet basics and staying safe |
| *Nerdy Birdy Tweets* | Aaron Reynolds | Online friendships and kindness |
| *Hello Ruby: Expedition to the Internet* | Linda Liukas | How the internet works |
| *Tek: The Modern Cave Boy* | Patrick McDonnell | Screen time balance |

## Teens (Ages 13+)

| Title | Author | Topic |
|-------|--------|-------|
| *Little Brother* | Cory Doctorow | Novel: surveillance, privacy, hacking |
| *Homeland* | Cory Doctorow | Sequel to *Little Brother* |
| *The Smart Girl's Guide to Privacy* | Violet Blue | Practical privacy and online safety |

## Free Online Resources

- [Childnet: Smartie the Penguin & Digiduck stories](https://www.childnet.com/) – free story books for young children

- [Common Sense Education: Digital Citizenship](https://www.commonsense.org/education/digital-citizenship) – free lessons by grade
